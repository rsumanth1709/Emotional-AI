# Crisis Support Guidance and Safety Planning

## Acute Crisis Intervention Principles
A mental health crisis occurs when an individual's emotional pain, distress, or sense of hopelessness overwhelms their internal coping resources and available support systems. In moments of acute despair or suicidal ideation, thinking often becomes narrowly constricted (cognitive tunneling), making permanent solutions appear as the only escape from temporary pain. Crucially, crisis states are acute and time-limited. Immediate intervention focuses on safety, connection, and reducing lethal means.

## Personal Crisis Safety Plan Steps
1. **Recognize Warning Signs**: Identify personal cues indicating an escalating crisis (e.g., intense agitation, social isolation, sleeplessness, thoughts of wanting to give up).
2. **Employ Somatic Distraction**: Change physical environment, run cold water over hands, or sit in a safe open room with a family member or friend.
3. **Contact a Designated Trusted Person**: Reach out to a specific named friend, family member, or mentor who provides calm, non-judgmental listening.
4. **Remove Immediate Means**: Ensure the living space is safe by removing or locking away items that could be used for self-harm.
