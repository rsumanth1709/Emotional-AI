# When and How to Seek Professional Mental Health Support

## Indicators That Professional Support is Recommended
While self-care, mindfulness, and healthy habits provide important emotional maintenance, they are not substitutes for clinical mental health diagnosis or professional therapy. Professional consultation is strongly indicated when:
- Emotional distress, overwhelming worry, or low mood persists continuously for more than two to three weeks.
- Symptoms significantly interfere with work performance, academic responsibilities, relationships, or personal hygiene.
- Coping mechanisms turn toward self-destructive habits, extreme withdrawal, or substance misuse.
- Experiencing panic attacks that disrupt normal mobility or create persistent agoraphobic fear of future attacks.
- Thoughts of self-harm, wanting to disappear, or feelings that life is not worth living emerge.

## Distinguishing Mental Health Professionals
Understanding the roles of different providers helps individuals navigate care effectively:
- **Clinical Psychologists**: Hold doctoral degrees (PhD or PsyD) and specialize in psychological assessment, diagnostic evaluation, and evidence-based psychotherapy (e.g., CBT, ACT, DBT, psychodynamic therapy). They generally do not prescribe medication.
- **Psychiatrists**: Medical doctors (MD or DO) specializing in psychiatry. They assess biological, physical, and psychological factors, and can prescribe, manage, and monitor psychiatric medications.
- **Licensed Counselors / Therapists (LPC, LMFT, LCSW)**: Master's level clinicians offering individual, couple, or family counseling, focused on life adjustments, coping mechanisms, and emotional processing.

## How to Prepare for an Initial Therapy Session
1. Write down primary concerns, recent triggers, and symptoms (including sleep quality and appetite).
2. Note any family history of mental health conditions or physical health diagnoses.
3. Prepare practical questions: What therapeutic modality do you use? How will we measure progress? What can I expect between sessions?
